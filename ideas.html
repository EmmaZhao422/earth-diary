40 moments worth pinning

A map diary gets good when it holds more than holidays. The pins you will be happiest to find in ten years are mostly small ones. Here are forty to get you started — pick one and drop it now.

Open the map

Firsts

  • The first place you lived on your own.
  • Where you learned to ride a bike, swim or drive.
  • The first concert, match or show you went to.
  • The first meal you cooked for someone else — and where you bought the ingredients.
  • The classroom, office or shop floor of your first day.
  • The first time you saw the sea, snow, or a desert.
  • The airport or station where a big trip began.
  • Where you met a person who is still in your life.

Food and drink

  • The restaurant you would take a visitor to without thinking.
  • The late-night place that was open when nothing else was.
  • A street stall you still think about.
  • The café where you got real work done.
  • A meal that went wrong in a memorable way.
  • The bakery, noodle shop or market stall where they know your order.
  • A dish you tried for the first time, and what you expected it to taste like.
  • The table where a celebration happened.

Ordinary places

  • The bench, step or corner where you wait for a friend.
  • Your walk to school, with a pin at the spot you always noticed.
  • The library seat, studio or court you think of as yours.
  • A shop that has closed, pinned where it used to be.
  • The bus stop of a commute you no longer make.
  • A view from a window you looked out of for years.
  • Where you go when you need to think.
  • The park where the dog, or the kids, or you, ran in circles.

People

  • A grandparent’s kitchen.
  • The place of a conversation that changed your mind about something.
  • Where you said a hard goodbye.
  • A friend’s old apartment and what you used to do there.
  • The spot of a photo everyone in the family knows.
  • Where you were when you got very good news.
  • A reunion, and who travelled furthest for it.
  • A stranger who helped you, and where.

Out in the world

  • A summit, with the time you reached it.
  • The best sunrise or sunset you remember, and who you were with.
  • A wrong turn that turned out well.
  • The furthest north, south, east and west you have ever been.
  • A place that was nothing like the pictures — better or worse.
  • Where you were caught in weather.
  • A museum room, a single painting, a single tree.
  • The place you want to go back to most. Pin it, and write why.
A tip for filling in the past: you do not need the exact date. Pick the first of the month, or of the year, and write what you remember. A rough pin is worth more than a perfect one you never drop.
