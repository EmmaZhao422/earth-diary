Privacy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Short version: your diary stays on your device. Earth Diary has no accounts and no server that receives your pins, text, photos or videos.

What is stored, and where

  • Your memories — pins, titles, dates, diary text, tags, photos and videos — are saved in your browser’s on-device storage (IndexedDB) for this site. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your settings — language, map style and similar switches, and your answer to the cookie banner — are saved in your browser’s local storage.

You can remove all of it at any time with Settings → Delete everything, or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Services the map contacts

To draw a map and look up place names, your browser requests data directly from these services. As with any website request, they receive your IP address and browser details.

  • OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org) — map tiles, fonts and icons for the area of the map you are looking at.
  • Photon by komoot (photon.komoot.io) — receives the text you type in the search box, and the coordinates of a spot when you tap the map or drop a pin, in order to return place names. It does not receive your diary text, photos or videos.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website and, like any web host, processes requests for its files.

If you choose “Use my location”, your browser asks for your permission and gives the position to the page on your device. It is used to place the pin and to look up the place name as described above.

Advertising

Earth Diary is funded by advertising. When ads are shown, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, and learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can opt out of some other vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • When the site shows a cookie banner, choosing “Only essential” requests non-personalized ads.

Ads have no access to your memories: those are in your browser’s storage for this site, not on a server, and are never included in ad requests.

Affiliate links

Some links, such as “Stays nearby” or “Things to do” inside an opened memory, may be affiliate links. If you click one, the destination site receives the place name used for the search and may set its own cookies; if you make a purchase, Earth Diary may earn a commission. These links are marked as sponsored.

Analytics

The site may count page views to understand how many people use it. If analytics that use cookies are enabled, they follow your choice in the cookie banner.

Children

Earth Diary is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them — or from anyone, since memories are stored only on the user’s device.

Changes and contact

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions and requests can be raised through the project’s GitHub issues.
