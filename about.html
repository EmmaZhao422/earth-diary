A map of your life, not just your trips

Earth Diary is a world map you fill in yourself. Tap any place on Earth, drop a small animated pin there, and keep the story inside it: what happened, how it felt, and the photos and videos you took.

Open the map

Why it exists

Photos pile up in a camera roll sorted by date. Notes sit in a notes app. The place where something happened — the thing that actually brings a memory back — gets lost. Earth Diary puts place first. Open the map and your life is laid out on it: the ramen counter at 1 a.m., the bench where you got the phone call, the trailhead, the first apartment.

It is meant to be used for small things as much as big trips. A pin for a restaurant two streets from home counts just as much as a pin for another continent.

What you can do

  • 🗺️Pin any spot on EarthThe map is built from OpenStreetMap, so it goes all the way down to single cafés, restaurants, shops, parks and peaks. Tap a place name to pin that exact place.
  • 📖Keep a diary in each pinGive it a title, a date, a mood and as much text as you like. Add tags so you can find “firsts” or “food” later.
  • 📷Attach photos and videosAdd as many as you want to a pin and flip through them in a full-screen viewer.
  • 🐱Choose a characterFourteen hand-drawn pin characters that bob, blink and bounce, in ten colors.
  • 🪪Watch your passport growCountries, cities and continents are counted for you, and countries you have been to light up on the globe.
  • 🧵See your journeyTurn on the journey line to connect your pins in the order they happened.

Private by design

There is no account and no sign-up. Your pins, diary entries, photos and videos are saved inside your own browser, on your own device, and are not uploaded to a server. The trade-off is that your diary lives on one device: to move it or keep a safe copy, export a backup file from Settings and import it wherever you want it. The privacy page lists exactly which outside services the map talks to (map tiles and place search) and what they see.

How it is funded

Earth Diary is free to use. It is paid for by advertising on the site and by affiliate links: when an opened memory offers links such as “Stays nearby”, and you book something through one, the site may earn a small commission at no cost to you. Ads never see the contents of your diary, because the diary never leaves your device.

Built on open data and open source

The map is drawn with MapLibre GL JS using free vector tiles from OpenFreeMap, which are made from OpenStreetMap data contributed by millions of volunteers. Place search uses the open-source Photon geocoder. If a café is missing or misnamed on the map, you can fix it for everyone by editing OpenStreetMap.

The source code of Earth Diary is public on GitHub. Bug reports and ideas are welcome there.
